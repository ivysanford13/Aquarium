ppm ⇄ mg/L converter

Converts using your water's density, so it's exact for saltwater too, not just a relabel.
0 ppm = 0 mg/L
mg/L = ppm × density (kg/L). In freshwater the two units come out essentially equal; saltwater is denser, so the same ppm reads slightly higher in mg/L.
